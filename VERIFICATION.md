## Changes made after the check
| Item / rule | Before | Waste Wizard says | Change |
|---|---|---|---|
| Flow step 6 | Black foam and foam packing peanuts → Garbage | Styrofoam peanuts → Blue Bin (in a tied clear bag); black plastic (bags, coffee lids) → Blue Bin | Step 6 now only asks about cups, cutlery or bags labelled compostable |
| Foam takeout container | Reason said black foam is garbage | Foam packaging → Blue Bin | Removed the black foam note |
| AA batteries | No prep step | Household hazardous waste; tape the positive terminal | Added `tape` step |
| "Phone battery or vape" | One item | Lithium-ion batteries → HHW. Vapes with removable batteries: battery to HHW, rest to Garbage. Built-in battery → HHW | Renamed to "Lithium-ion battery (phone or laptop)" (`lithium-battery`) to avoid the vape split |
| Expired medication | Pharmacy take-back | Household hazardous waste | Reason now says HHW (pharmacies as an extra option) |
| Wine bottle | Return for deposit | Not accepted; return to The Beer Store | Reason names The Beer Store |

## Confirmed as-is
Plastic water bottle, pop can, glass jar (and metal lid), cereal box, milk carton, flyers, banana, fruit core, eggshell, bones → as listed.
Paper coffee cup, chip bag, toothpaste tube, foam takeout, plastic bag → Blue. Tissue, coffee grounds, diaper, dog poop, food-soiled pizza box → Green (clean pizza box → Blue).
Compostable paper cup, baby wipes, face mask, mug, drinking glass, clothing, toys, wooden chopsticks, hair → Garbage.
Chargers → Electronics. Paint, syringes, propane, fluorescent tubes, spray paint → Household Hazardous Waste.

Not in Waste Wizard search: paper towel soiled with cleaning chemicals. Source: City of Toronto Green Bin page ("if soiled with chemicals such as cleaning products, place in the Garbage Bin").
